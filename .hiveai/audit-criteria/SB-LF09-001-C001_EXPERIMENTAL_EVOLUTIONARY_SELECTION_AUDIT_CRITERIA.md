# SB-LF09-001-C001 — Experimental Evolutionary Selection — Strict Audit Criteria

PASS requires a versioned evolutionary-selection prototype that is explicitly opt-in, deterministic for the same accepted input/policy/seed, bounded by finite population/generation/evaluation budgets, and reproducible from immutable candidate/evidence lineage. Missing, unavailable, invalid, duplicate, or cross-candidate evidence must fail closed. The selection prototype must not alter accepted source/art bytes, bypass M03/M04/M05/M07 acceptance, promote output to production, or add network/provider/telemetry/API-key/runtime HTTP behavior. Tests must prove positive selection, replay determinism, budget enforcement, opt-in isolation, invalid/unavailable evidence rejection, lineage binding, duplicate prevention, and no production-promotion path. Full retained regressions, compileall, Godot headless boot, diff-check, and protected tracker/audit checks are required; unavailable native or owner gates must remain explicitly unavailable.
